03 / Career evolution

From designing things to shaping what gets made.

The thread through my career is not a move away from design. It is an expansion of responsibility around it — from making the work, to understanding the problem, directing the work and ultimately owning what happens around it.

01

MAKE

My foundation was hands-on: graphic design, visual communication, product graphics, interface work, prototyping and physical execution.

Graphic design · Product design · Visual systems
02

UNDERSTAND

I moved upstream into research and problem framing — understanding users, markets and context before deciding what should be designed.

User research · Competition · Opportunity framing
03

DIRECT

Design became a shared direction: translating briefs, shaping decisions, guiding designers and engineers, and aligning clients around the work.

Design direction · Client leadership · Team guidance
04

OWN

Today I work across the conditions that determine whether good design actually gets delivered — people, priorities, timelines, vendors and commercial realities.

Project ownership · Delivery · Commercial context
